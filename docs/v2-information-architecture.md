# Ghost Cart v2 information architecture

## Mobile navigation

### Home

- Primary `Ghost something` action.
- Active cooldowns that are nearly ready.
- Recent resolved decisions.
- Secondary Most Ghosted Today module.
- Optional route to marketplace discovery.

### Cooldowns

- Ready to decide.
- Cooling now.
- Snoozed.
- Resolution history filter.

### Ghost +

- Manual capture.
- Paste product URL.
- Add screenshot placeholder until import processing is available.
- Category, trigger, amount, and cooling-period selection.

### Progress

- Money Kept.
- Almost Spent.
- Cooling value.
- Bought intentionally.
- Outcome and trigger patterns.
- Ghost Receipt history.

### Profile

- Account and privacy.
- Cooling notifications.
- Separate lunch/dinner/late-night nudges.
- Quiet hours and pause.
- Accessibility and theme.
- Ghost membership card.

## Content priority

The interface should answer these questions in order:

1. What am I tempted by right now?
2. What is waiting for my decision?
3. What did I decide?
4. What am I learning?
5. What are other people Ghosting?

Marketplace promotions and sponsored simulations never outrank the first four questions.
