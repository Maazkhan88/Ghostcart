# Ghost Cart website product specification

## Website goals

1. Explain Ghost Cart in one screen.
2. Make the idea tangible through a working simulation.
3. Build trust through unambiguous safety language.
4. Collect waitlist intent without claiming persistence until a backend is connected.

## Sections

1. Cinematic dark hero.
2. Editorial light “How it works.”
3. Dark interactive demo.
4. Light comparison/benefit story.
5. Dark sample insights dashboard.
6. Light example almost-buy scenarios.
7. Accessible FAQ.
8. Dark waitlist CTA and artistic footer.

## Demo behavior

- Add an item through a visible button, double-click, or keyboard.
- Press and hold to activate a short cooling state.
- Remove/undo an item.
- Complete a Fake Checkout without entering any payment details.
- Generate a Ghost Receipt summary and update the demo item count.
- Clearly label every result as simulated.
